# StaffFlow HR

**Specialized HR operations and people consulting for international schools and SMEs across the Gulf.**

We turn messy spreadsheets, unclear leave policies, staff intent chaos, and contract cycles into clean systems that schools and small companies can actually run.

> Niche first. Products first. Trust first. Scale only on demand.

---

## Why StaffFlow exists

International schools and Gulf SMEs live on spreadsheets for:
- Staff intent-to-return tracking
- Certificate and letter requests
- Demographics & nationality reports
- Leave policies and handbooks
- Contract cycles and expat HR admin

Large consultancies are too expensive and too generic. Local freelancers often lack process. StaffFlow sits in the middle: practical, affordable, and deeply familiar with the region’s realities.

---

## The three-layer model (start here)

### Layer 1 — Digital Products ($10–$50)
Polished Google Sheets templates sold on Gumroad / Etsy:

1. **Staff Intent-to-Return Tracker** (start here — you already know the process)
2. Certificate & Letter Request Tracker
3. Staff Demographics Dashboard
4. Leave Policy & Handbook Starter Pack

Zero inventory. Instant delivery. Tests willingness to pay.

### Layer 2 — Fixed-Fee Services ($200–$800)
**HR Health Check**: Review contracts, leave policy, and records → short findings report + prioritized fix list.

### Layer 3 — Retainers ($300–$1,500 / month)
Ongoing HR administration. Routine work done by trained remote associates in Nepal under your supervision.

**Rule**: Do not open Layer 3 until Layers 1 + 2 produce steady income and testimonials.

## Critical guardrails

1. **Employment contract** — Check outside-work / conflict-of-interest clauses. Get written permission if needed. Never use current employer’s data, policies, or templates.
2. **Kuwait freelancing rules** — Confirm licensing / visa implications before taking local paying clients. Digital products to overseas buyers are usually safer to start.
3. **Data security** — Client employee data is sensitive. Role-based access, no personal devices, deletion protocols, NDAs + DPAs.
4. **Confidentiality & quality** — Senior review before anything reaches a client. Clear contracts always.
